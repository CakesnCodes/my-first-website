Cloud Computing In Education

Cloud computing can in enhance the education sector in various ways, from providing students with a digital library of works regarding various topics, allowing them to collaborate with each other on group projects with ease, and even let them attend classes, all within the comfort of their homes (matellio, 2024).

The use of cloud computing in education has been especially relevant and useful during and after The COVID-19 Pandemic for it's scalability, convenience, and cost-efficiency (Gupta, 2025). Which is notably valuable in a world that is inundated with new and rapidly changing technology.

As someone who has spent a majority of their life indoors, both recreationally and educationally, I have rather mixed feelings about the presence of The Cloud in education. Yes, A lot of the benefits outweigh some of its downsides; namely the Internet Dependency and Initial Setup costs, but being in a class that primarily used Virtual classrooms makes me feel like I'm living through The Pandemic again, but worse.

I am frankly in need of an environment that allows me to interact with my peers in-person. I understand that sometimes in order to communicate with others, I have to be the one that initiate the conversation, however an environment where everyone is represented by a profile image instead of their face makes me feel less incentivized to do so.

I acknowledge why I can't attend physical classes, and that its practically impossible for a class with students from around the world, but I miss the classroom environment and having a clear distinction from my "school life" and "personal life".

Cloud computing is a technology model that allows people to use infrastructure and technologies over a computer network in order to accomplish various goals, including file storage, running servers, or sharing documents. All without the hassle of having to purchase and maintain the hardware

2)

  • Being to run things on-demand by yourself without needing intervention from your provider
  • Customers can access the cloud through WIFI or other standard network mechanisms
  • The Infrastructure is shared between multiple customers
  • Allows for rapid up or downscaling when needed
  • Usage of resources is monitored and measured by the provider

3)

  • Private cloud - Organization only cloud that may or may not be on the premises
  • Public Cloud - The infrastructure is available to the public
  • Community Cloud - Accessed by a closed circle of organizations that require similar infrastructure
  • Hybrid Cloud- connected distinct cloud environments
